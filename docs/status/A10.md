## Pass 1 (PR #21, merged)

## What was done

- `src/cities/berlin/BerlinScene.tsx` — replaces the shared graybox. Everything solid is derived from `definition.blockers`; nothing collidable is rendered outside a declared blocker rectangle. `definition.ts` is untouched (no socket / district / blocker changes).
  - **Brandenburg Gate** (`brandenburg-gate` blocker): plinth, six Doric column pairs with capitals, entablature, attic, and a stylised quadriga (four horses, chariot, charioteer, staff) on top. Bollard line + plaza paving in front.
  - **TV Tower** (`tv-tower` blocker): base pavilion + pedestal inside the footprint, a single tapered shaft mesh (36 tall) and a silver sphere with equator band and antenna. The shaft is thinner than the footprint so it never leaves the collision rect.
  - **Painted wall** (`gallery-wall` blocker): concrete wall with cap and end pillars; the +Z face carries seeded abstract murals (blocks, circles, slanted bars in the arcade palette). No text, no copied art.
  - **Altbau** (`altbau-a/b/c`): each blocker is split into 3–5 houses with cream/pastel façades, cornices, dark mansard roofs, chimneys and instanced windows.
  - **Plattenbau**: slab body, seam lines, window rows, coloured balcony panels.
  - **Kiosk row**: three coloured kiosks with awnings, counters and signs.
  - Ground: grey paving, roads with tram rails and broken lane markings, red bike lanes on sidewalks, cosmetic lawns (Tiergarten side + riverside strip), street trees, lamp posts, bikes, socket pads with gold rings, lavender backdrop blocks outside the play bounds for depth.
- Reuse: 7 shared geometries, 4 shared `MeshLambertMaterial`s; everything repeated goes through five `InstancedMesh` batches (planes, boxes, cylinders, spheres, rings). Cosmetic placement uses `createRng(hashSeed('berlin-cosmetic', seed))`; no `Math.random()`, no per-frame React state, no network.
- `quality: 'low'`: same layout and blockers; fewer trees/lamps/bikes, no windows, chimneys, mural detail, awnings or bollards.
- `src/cities/berlin/definition.test.ts` — unique ids, one blocker per landmark, flood-fill reachability from spawn to all nine sockets at `PLAYER_RADIUS`, and open corridors on both sides of the gate and tower squares.

## Known issues

- The TV-tower sphere (y≈38) is off-screen unless you stand well south of the tower; the shaft reads as the silhouette in normal play.
- Frame rate not measured on a real GPU in this environment; cost is bounded by construction (5 instanced draws + 2 tower meshes).
