# A3 — Paris status

## State: PR_OPEN (branch `feat/paris`, one PR into `main`)

## What was done

- `src/cities/paris/definition.ts` — authored Paris layout as pure data: north–south river (four `river-*` blockers with gaps at three bridge roads), two boulevards, three east–west avenues, 20 façade blocks, and the three landmark blockers (`eiffel`, `louvre`, `cafe`). Landmark, district and socket ids are unchanged. Spawn moved to `[18, 0, 58]` (arrival quay, art-direction §Paris). `cafe-b` moved from `[42,0,44]` to `[42,0,46]` so it clears the café footprint by ≥3 units.
- `src/cities/paris/ParisScene.tsx` — replaces the shared graybox. Everything is derived from `definition`: river meshes from the `river-*` blockers, bridges from roads that overlap the river, façades split from every other blocker (never outside the blocker rect), roads/plazas from `roads`, landmarks from the `eiffel`/`louvre`/`cafe` blockers, socket pads + gold rings from `sockets`. Trees and lamps are placed by a seeded RNG (`hashSeed('paris-cosmetic', seed)`) and rejected if inside any blocker grown by 2.5, inside a road, or within 3 units of a socket — so no prop ever contradicts collision or blocks a target.
- Landmarks: Eiffel Tower (four tapered struts, two platforms, spire, ~32 tall), Louvre (two wings + courtyard, transparent glass pyramid), café (low frontage, red/white striped awning, terrace tables/chairs).
- Reuse: 8 shared geometries and 14 shared `MeshStandardMaterial`s at module scope; all repeated props (tree canopies/trunks, lamp posts/heads, windows, chimneys, socket pads/rings, roof ridges) are `InstancedMesh`. Lighting comes only from the app's shared hemisphere + directional lights; "baked" feel is done with flat/low-roughness materials and darker roof/side tones.
- `quality: 'low'`: same layout and blockers; 28 instead of 80 prop clusters, no windows/chimneys, no Eiffel cross-braces/platform rails, no café chairs.
- `src/cities/paris/definition.test.ts` — flood-fill over a 1-unit grid using `buildCollisionWorld` at `PLAYER_RADIUS`: spawn is clear, every socket is clear (3-unit radius) and reachable from spawn, ≥2 bridges connect both banks.

## Perf pass 2 (perf/paris-2)

Integration reported Paris at 1.67 fps after the fidelity passes. Fix: per-batch spatial chunking (48-unit cells, per-chunk bounding spheres, frustum culling on) so off-screen scenery is never submitted, plus a hard instance cut (side-face ribbon windows, continuous top-floor balconies, paired awnings, fewer dormers/benches/bollards/backdrop tiles, no ripples/chimney pots/bench seats/chair backs). Standard instances 2234 -> ~1700; low unchanged. Local SwiftShader: perf.spec Paris 21.3 -> 31.6 fps; probe arrival 26 -> 39 fps, low 55 -> 57 fps (vsync-capped). Draw calls rose from 10 to ~60/frame (one per visible chunk) — cheap on a CPU rasteriser, culling wins.

## Known issues

- The Eiffel Tower top is clipped by the fixed camera when the player stands right at its base — a consequence of its height vs the `[0,52,36]` offset; it reads well from any distance.
- Façade windows are placed on the long axis only; short sides are plain.
- The 3-unit socket clearance in the test is my own threshold; A6's placement validation may want a different one.
