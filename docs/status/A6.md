## What was done

- **`src/content/validate.ts`** — content validator reading only the city definition's own geometry:
  - flood-fill reachability from the city spawn on a 1-unit grid with blockers expanded by `PLAYER_RADIUS` (same rule as `moveCircle`), cached per `CityDefinition`;
  - per-socket checks: ground anchor (y=0), inside walkable bounds, outside expanded blockers, anchor **and the whole pickup disc** (`PICKUP_RADIUS − PLAYER_RADIUS` ring) reachable, socket inside broad / narrowed / fine regions, narrowed ⊂ broad;
  - per-target checks: references, socket ↔ district agreement, no template fields, clue ≤ 90 chars, hints ≤ 110 chars, tier 1 names the city, tier 2 names the district/landmark, any compass word in tier 2 matches the real bearing from spawn, tier 3 describes the patch and is longer than tier 2, free clue never names the city;
  - per-level / catalog checks: two distinct targets, both cities, six distinct targets over three trials, unique seeds and IDs, unsigned-32-bit seed, increasing medals.
  - Two severities: `error` (unreachable / unfindable / broken reference) and `warning` (findable but worth a human look: clearance < 3u, fine patch spilling past the broad region, socket > 12u from its landmark, clue naming the landmark).
- **`resolveObjectives`** now re-validates the chosen socket and **throws** on any error-severity issue, so a bad socket can never silently ship. Selection algorithm unchanged (sorted socket IDs, mulberry32 seeded by FNV-1a over `level.seed, level.version, targetId`) — golden outputs below are identical to the bootstrap resolver.
- **Content review** (`targets.ts`): clues for `paris-crescent` and `giza-beetle` no longer name the landmark/district (they were making the paid tier-2 hint worthless); tier-2 hints now name the district and its compass direction from the spawn crossroads; tier-3 hints rewritten so each is true for all three candidate sockets (e.g. `giza-crown` no longer says "circle the pyramid's base" when `pyramid-b` sits 14u away). No AI Studio output was used; wording is hand-revised from the starter table.
- **Tests** (`tests/unit/content.test.ts`, 12 tests): zero errors + exact known-warning allowlist; every candidate socket (18) reachable; golden socket per committed seed, stable under target/definition order; both cities in every trial; hint escalation; negative tests on a mock city (sealed-off socket, socket inside blocker, obstructed pickup disc, socket outside regions, resolver throwing, leaking clue, wrong compass direction, non-escalating tiers, template fields, single-city trial, reused target).
