# A8 — Rome status

## State: PR_OPEN (branch `devin/*-a8-rome`, one PR into `main`)

## What was done

- `src/cities/rome/RomeScene.tsx` — replaces the shared graybox. Everything is derived from
  `definition`; no blocker, socket or district in `definition.ts` was changed.
  - **Colosseum** (`colosseum` blocker, hero): three tiers of pillared arcades on an oval kept strictly
    inside the collision rectangle, entablature bands, dark arch openings, sand arena floor, and a
    broken fourth-storey wall that survives on ~55% of the circumference (theta-limited cylinder +
    ring, two single meshes). The rectangle itself is a travertine plinth so collision and visuals agree.
  - **Trevi-style fountain** (`fountain`): balustrade exactly on the blocker edge with corner posts,
    big basin with cyan water, baroque backdrop wall with columns and central arch, bronze figure,
    upper basin and spouts.
  - **Trattoria** (`trattoria`): ochre building, pantile hip roof, ground-floor arches, green/white
    striped awning, terrace tables under pink/yellow/cyan umbrellas (walk-through decoration in the
    landmark's front plaza).
  - **Insulae** (`insula-*`): each blocker split by a seeded RNG into 2–4 houses of varying height,
    warm ochre/terracotta plaster, terracotta hip roofs with eave trim, arches on the +Z ground floor,
    windows with green shutters on visible outer faces, occasional chimney.
  - **Forum ruin** (`forum-ruin`): raised platform, standing and broken columns with bases and
    capitals, a lintel, a fallen drum and rubble.
  - **Cypress row** (`cypress-row`): kerbed lawn with two lines of cypresses.
  - Roads become cobbled lanes with kerbs; sockets get a pavement disc and cream ring.
  - Cosmetic props (cypresses, stone pines, lamps) use `hashSeed('rome-cosmetic', seed)` and are
    rejected inside any blocker grown by 2.5, any landmark footprint grown by 6, roads, or within 5
    units of a socket or spawn.
- Render budget: 9 `InstancedMesh` draws (plane, box, cylinder, sphere, cone, hip roof, socket ring,
  open shell, tier annulus) + 2 single meshes for the attic; 3 shared Lambert materials; no
  transparency; no per-frame work. `quality: 'low'` halves Colosseum pillars, skips windows, shutters,
  arches, umbrellas, rubble, lamps and most trees.
